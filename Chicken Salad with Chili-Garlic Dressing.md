# Chicken Salad with Chili-Garlic Dressing

Makes six servings.  The chicken  and the dressing  can be made a day in advance
and stored in the refrigerator overnight.


## Ingredients & Instructions

### The Chicken

- 1 C chicken broth
- 1/2 C soy sauce
- 1/3 C light corn syrup*
- 1/4 C ginger
- 1/4 C orange juice
- 1 T orange zest
- 1/2 tsp Chinese five-spice
- 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper

Chop the ginger. Mix all the ingredients in a large skillet and bring to a boil,
then simmer for five minutes more.

- 6 chicken breasts

Add the chicken breasts.  Simmer for eight minutes, then flip the chicken, cover
the skillet,  turn off the heat,  and let sit for 30 more minutes.  Transfer the
chicken to a plate to cool, then refrigerate until chilled.


### The Dressing

- 3 T poaching liquid
- 2 T chili-garlic sauce
- 1/2 C light corn syrup*
- salt & pepper

Mix all ingredients in a small bowl.  Season with salt and pepper.


### Assembly

- 2 C jicama
- 10 C mixed greens
- the dressing

Cut the jicama into matchsticks. Mix it with the greens in a large bowl and toss
with enough of the dressing to coat lightly.

- the chicken
- green onion (optional)
- radish sprouts (optional)

Divide the greens among six plates. Cut the chicken into 1/4-inch slices and put
it on top of the greens, one breast per plate.  Garnish with green onions and/or
radish sprouts.  Serve with the remaining dressing on the side.


## Notes

I requested this for my high school graduation dinner, and that particular batch
ended up being very, very spicy... It seems to mainly depend on the chili-garlic
sauce you use, so if you want to be safe,  add that to the dressing one teaspoon
at a time until you get to your desired level of spiciness.
