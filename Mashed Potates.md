# Mashed Potates

## Ingredients & Instructions

- 4 lb yukon gold potatoes

Peel the potatoes,  then chop them.  Boil them in salted water  until soft, then
drain.

- 1 C milk or cream
- 1/3 C butter
- salt

Mash the potatoes with the other ingredients.  Salt to taste.


## Notes

The recipe I based this on has you heat the milk before adding it - I'm not sure
why.  It also has you  add the milk slowly  so you have better control  over the
consistency.  My first (half) batch didn't bother with any of that,  and I got a
very soft and smooth result with 2 lb of potatoes, 1/2 C milk, and 1/3 C butter.

You can also add  some extra flavoring.  Try boiling  a couple cloves  of garlic
along with the potatoes, or add grated cheese and/or sour cream while mashing.
