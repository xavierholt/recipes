# Miso Cod

## Ingredients & Instructions

- 1 lb skin-on black cod
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 T sake

Cut the fish into 2-inch strips, leaving the skin on. Sprinkle with salt to draw
out extra moisture.  Let sit for about 30 minutes, then rinse with sake.

- 6 T white miso or saikyo miso
- 1 T sugar (if using white miso)
- 3 T mirin
- 3 T sake

Mix these ingredients to make a marinade.  Marinate the fish in the refrigerator
for 2 to 3 days.

Preheat oven to 400 F.  Wipe marinade off of the fish,  then place the fish skin
side up on a baking sheet.  Bake until the skin is nicely browned,  which should
take about 20 minutes.  Serve with rice.


## Notes

This is another of our favorites from [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/black-cod-with-miso/).

We first discovered this dish at Roka Akor in San Francisco, where they serve it
with a creamy lemon-sesame sauce.
