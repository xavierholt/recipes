# Pesto

## Ingredients & Instructions

- 1 oz basil leaves
- 1/4 C toasted pine nuts
- 1/4 C grated parmesan
- 1/4 C olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp salt

Blend ALL the things.


## Notes

The herb here doesn't have to be basil. Some recipes use spinach or kale, and if
you're feeling boring, that's always an option.  I've also made this with shiso,
which was amazing - I think I liked it better than the original basil pesto.

I usually add a little more olive oil for extra smoothness.

Some recipes also add pepper (black or red) for a bit of spice.
