# Puff Pastry Pizza

## Ingredients & Instructions

- ~12 oz puff pastry

Preheat oven to 400 F.  Unroll the puff pastry on a baking sheet. Prick all over
with a fork.  Bake for 10 minutes, or until light golden brown.

- 1 C pizza sauce
- 1/2 lb mozarella
- toppings

Top with pizza sauce and shredded mozarella.  Add any "sturdy" toppings you want
at this time (things that will benefit from some time in the oven). These can go
above or  below the mozarella  as you see fit.  Keep baking  until the cheese is
nicely melted.

- more toppings

Add any "fragile" toppings at the end.  Slice and serve.


## Notes

The  amounts here are  very flexible.  I've always used a  13.2 oz  pack of puff
pastry that naturally unrolls to about a 9" x 14" rectangle (or around 11" x 17"
if I move it  and it stretches).  But I've seen recipes  ranging from  8 oz puff
pastry (rolled to 10" x 15") with 1 cup of pizza sauce all the way to over 16 oz
puff pastry (rolled to 10" by 13") with 2/3 cup pizza sauce.


### Topping Suggestions

Mix and match!  Some sturdy topping ideas:

- garlic
- diced ham
- bacon bits (pre-cooked)
- sun-dried tomatoes
- roasted garlic
- pepperoni
- parmesan

Some fragile topping ideas:

- fresh basil
- olive oil
- finishing salt
- chili oil
