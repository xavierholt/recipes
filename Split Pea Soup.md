# Split Pea Soup

## Ingredients & Instructions

- 1 onion
- 3 ribs celery
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 T butter

In a dutch oven, saute the vegetables until the onion is just golden.

- 1 lb cooked ham
- 1 lb dried split peas
- 4 C chicken broth
- 2 1/2 C water
- 1 bay leaf

Cut the ham into small cubes.  Add the ham and all the other ingredients. Simmer
until the peas are tender, about 1 hour and 15 minutes.

- salt
- pepper

Season to taste with salt and pepper, then serve.
